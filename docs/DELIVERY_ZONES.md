## 2. Delivery Packaging & Temperature Control

- **Handi & Karahi**: Sealed food-grade insulated aluminum containers preserving heat up to 60 minutes.
- **BBQ & Grills**: Perforated foil steam trays to maintain crisp char without getting soggy.
- **Naan & Tandoor**: Breathable craft paper bags to prevent moisture trapping.
- **Raita & Chutney**: Chilled sealed containers placed in dedicated separate cool pouches.
