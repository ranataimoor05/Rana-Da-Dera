This document inventories all 18 authentic dishes, categories, verified prices in PKR, and combos preserved from the legacy Rana Da Dera site.

## 1. Categories

1. **Karahi & Handi (`karahi-handi`)** — Slow-cooked desi specials in clay pots and iron woks.
2. **BBQ & Grills (`bbq`)** — Charcoal-grilled marinated kebabs, boti, and chops.
3. **Rice & Biryani (`rice`)** — Fragrant long-grain basmati biryani and pulao.
4. **Breads & Naan (`breads`)** — Tandoori breads, rogheni naan, and stuffed parathas.
5. **Beverages & Desserts (`beverages-desserts`)** — Traditional lassi, chai, kheer, and gulab jamun.
6. **Combos & Family Deals (`combos`)** — Curated feast platters for families and gatherings.
