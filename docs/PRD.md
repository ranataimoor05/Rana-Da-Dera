# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Rana Da Dera

## 1. Executive Summary
**Rana Da Dera** is a premier authentic Punjabi and Potohar fine-dining restaurant based in **Naseerabad, Rawalpindi, Pakistan**. This project establishes a modern, high-performance, responsive React + Vite + TypeScript web application providing seamless online food ordering, table reservations, live kitchen status tracking, and an operations administration portal.

## 2. Core Business Objectives
- **Online Food Ordering**: Deliver hot, thermal-packaged Karahi, Handi, BBQ, Pulao, and Tandoori naans across Naseerabad, Rawalpindi Cantt, Westridge, and surrounding areas.
- **Table Reservations**: Enable patrons to reserve dining slots, specify seating preferences (Indoor Royal Hall, Courtyard Takht, Private Family Cabin), and log special requests.
- **Brand Elevation**: Present a modern luxury visual identity featuring forest-green and dark-green primary colors, gold accents, and subtle glassmorphism surfaces.
- **Dual Language & Culture**: Provide full bilingual English and Urdu experiences with native Right-to-Left (RTL) typography.
- **Accurate Presentation**: Exact Pakistani rupee presentation style: `Rs. 1,250` with thousand separators.

## 3. Key Non-Negotiable Business Rules
- **No-Refund Policy**: All culinary preparations are perishable and made to order in live iron woks and tandoors. Orders are strictly non-refundable once confirmed and transmitted to the kitchen.
- **No Self-Service Order Editing**: Customers cannot edit submitted order items or quantities after checkout. Coordination is handled verbally via the Naseerabad kitchen helpline.
- **Operating Hours**: The live kitchen operates daily from **2:00 AM to 3:00 PM**. Pre-orders and scheduled deliveries outside active hours are supported.
- **Delivery Serviceability**: Configured delivery zones across Rawalpindi with specific delivery fees and minimum order requirements.

## 4. Target Personas
- **Local Family Diners**: Families seeking weekend or evening gatherings in a traditional Punjabi ambience.
- **Midnight & Dawn Feasters**: Patrons ordering 2:00 AM hot Nihari, Paye, and fresh naans after shifts or late-night travel.
- **Office & Corporate Dawats**: Corporate lunches in Saddar, Westridge, and Peshawar Road requiring group platter bundles.
- **Restaurant Operations & Staff**: Managers and staff monitoring the live kitchen queue and updating availability.
